# Dachshund Cottage keyword map

Every keyword group from the research list is assigned to **one** page, so pages never compete. Volumes are monthly US searches.

## How to use this map

1. **Check search intent** on google.com (signed out, US): listings in the results → the puppies page; articles → a guide.
2. **Watch Google Search Console → Queries** after 4–8 weeks and strengthen the intended page if Google picks another.
3. **Add first-hand evidence:** your own puppy and parent photos, videos, health records and real reviews. This is the core of E-E-A-T.
4. **Fill in the E-E-A-T details in Admin:** breeder name, story and photo (About), parents' health details (Our dachshunds), license (Verify us), phone and social links.
