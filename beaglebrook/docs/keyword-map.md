# Beagle Brook keyword map

Every keyword from the research file (`table_beagle_100rows_2026-10-29.csv`, 100 rows) is assigned to **one** page, so pages never compete with each other. Volumes are the monthly US search volumes from that file.

## How to use this map

1. **Check search intent before you judge a page.** Search each group's main keyword on google.com (signed out, US location) and look at the top 10. If Google shows product/category pages, the matching page here must be the listings page; if it shows articles, a guide. The assignments below follow that rule, but results change, so re-check the big groups every few months.
2. **Watch Google Search Console → Performance → Queries.** After 4–8 weeks you'll see which page Google shows for each search. If Google picks a different page from the one below, strengthen the intended page (title, H1, internal links) rather than creating a new one.
3. **Add first-hand evidence** to the pages that sell: your own puppy and parent photos, real health-test results, real reviews, and videos. That is what Google's helpful-content and E-E-A-T guidance rewards, and it is the one thing competing breeder sites can't copy.
4. **Don't create near-duplicate pages** for small wording changes ("beagle for sale" vs "beagles for sale"). One strong page per group wins.

**Puppy pages** (`/beagle-puppies-for-sale/{name}-{color}-{sex}`) are the product pages. They target long-tail searches like “lemon female beagle puppy” and carry the photos, price, availability, parents and delivery details buyers compare. Sold puppies stay visible for trust but are kept out of Google’s index automatically.

## Off-site work that moves rankings most

- **Google Business Profile** for Beagle Brook as a service-area business (Cincinnati + the states you deliver to). This is what shows in the map pack for “beagle puppies near me”.
- **Reviews** on Google from every family, linked from the Reviews page (Settings → Google & social → Google review link).
- **Breed-club and registry listings** (e.g. AKC Marketplace, if you qualify) and local directories: real links from relevant sites.
- **Short videos** of each litter on YouTube/Instagram/TikTok, linked to the puppy pages.
