**The pattern behind what ranks:** one page per search intent, the answer in the first sentence, live data with a visible “updated” time, comparison tables, an FAQ section, heavy internal linking, and a site with many pages on the same topic (topical authority). Everything here is built to that pattern.

## 2. Will it reach #1?

Honest answer:

- **Long-tail and price pages** (14K price per gram, 21K gold price, gold bar weight, 400 oz bar, gold biscuit weight, kilo/pound of gold, white gold price, mini gold coins…): **yes, realistic**, typically within 3–9 months once Google trusts the site, because the current results are weak.
- **Product-size pages** (1 gram gold bar, 1 oz gold bar…): page 1 is realistic. #1 needs backlinks, because APMEX and JM Bullion have years of them.
- **“gold bullion” itself**: possible but the hardest. Your keyword tool says KD 12, but the pages ranking belong to dealers with tens of thousands of linking domains. It needs everything below: time (6–18 months), real backlinks and strong trust signals. **No one can honestly guarantee #1.**

Gold sales count as “Your Money or Your Life” content to Google, so trust signals matter more here than on almost any other kind of site.

### Keywords deliberately not targeted

- **Other dealers' brand names** (apmex gold price, j m bullion gold bars, united states gold bureau, md bullion, lincoln coin and bullion, wholesale coins direct, cr coins, 321 gold, ajpm gold, golden eagle coin maryland, black lab bullion, malabar gold): those searchers want that company, and targeting another business's name invites trademark problems.
- **Off-topic “gold” searches** (teaching strategies gold, australian gold sunscreen, gold coast, gold dresses, bangles, rings, necklaces, grills, watches, sunglasses, cabinet handles, calacatta gold marble, fallout 76 gold bullion, gold dipped rose, gold vermeil, lyrics…): the people searching don't buy bullion. Ranking for them would send traffic that never buys and dilutes the site's topic in Google's eyes.
- **City-specific “gold price today near tucson / dallas / chicago / edison nj”**: one page per city with the same prices is what Google calls doorway pages, and it can get a site demoted. The main gold price page covers these searches.

## 4. What's built into the site (on-page)

- Keyword-first titles under 60 characters and meta descriptions of 140–160 characters, some with **live prices** (e.g. “A gram of gold is worth $129.00 today”), which improves click-through.
- The answer in the first sentence of every guide, for featured snippets.
- Live numbers rendered in the HTML with an “Updated” timestamp, so Google sees a fresh page. Sitemap `changefreq` is set to *hourly* for price pages.
- FAQ sections on almost every page, with FAQPage markup.
- Product markup with price, availability and shipping details for Google Shopping-style results.
- Internal linking: every guide links to the matching category and price pages using keyword anchors. Price pages cross-link all karats. The footer links every price page, category and policy page.
- A table of contents with jump links on long guides.
- An **Editorial Policy** page (who writes the guides, sources, how values are calculated, corrections) linked from every guide byline — an E-E-A-T signal Google’s quality raters look for.
- **Curated internal links**: every price page links to the 4–5 guides that explain it; every guide links to its closest guides and the full list of price pages; the footer links the most important guides site-wide.
- Trust pages: About, Editorial Policy, Contact, FAQ, How to Order, Payment Methods, Shipping & Returns, Authenticity Guarantee, Track Order, Privacy, Terms, AML/KYC, Cookies, Disclaimer and Accessibility.

## 5. What you need to do (this decides the ranking)

**Week 1**

1. **Fill in your real business details** in Admin → Settings: registered business name, phone number and address. For a gold seller this is the single biggest trust factor, for Google's quality raters and for buyers. Use the same name, address and phone everywhere online.
2. **Use an email on the same domain.** Orders currently go to `sales@goldbullion.online` while the site is `goldbullion-shop.com`. A mismatched domain looks suspicious to customers and hurts deliverability. Set up `sales@goldbullion-shop.com` (and forward the old address to it), then update Settings. Set SPF, DKIM and DMARC records for the sending domain, or order emails will land in spam.
3. **Google Search Console**: add the domain, paste the verification tag in Settings, submit `https://goldbullion-shop.com/sitemap.xml`, then use *URL inspection → Request indexing* for the home page, `/gold-price`, `/14k-gold-price`, `/gold-price-per-gram` and the category pages. Do the same in **Bing Webmaster Tools** (it can import from Search Console).
4. **Google Business Profile**, if you have a real business address. This gives local visibility for “gold and silver shops near me”.
5. Import your products. Category pages stay `noindex` until they have products.

**Months 1–6: links and reputation**

Content gets you into the results; backlinks and reputation decide the top spots for “gold bullion”.

- **Reviews**: ask every real customer for a review on Google and Trustpilot. Never buy or write fake reviews: Google and the FTC penalise it, and for a gold seller it destroys trust fast.
- **Citations**: list the business consistently on Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, BBB (accreditation if you qualify), Yelp, chamber of commerce and precious-metals directories.
- **Digital PR**: the live price pages are linkable assets. Pitch journalists and finance bloggers data-led stories, for example “What a kilo of gold is worth this year”, “The price of a 14K wedding band, then and now” or “How many gold bars fit in a suitcase”.
- **Guest articles** on personal-finance, coin-collecting and investing sites, linking to the guides (not just the home page).
- **Partnerships**: coin clubs, jewelers who need a bullion partner, and local finance or estate-planning professionals.
- Avoid paid link packages and private blog networks. Google catches them, and recovery takes months.

**Ongoing**

- Add 2–4 new guides a month around new long-tail questions that appear in Search Console (Performance → Queries with impressions but low position).
- Refresh the “Updated” date on guides when you improve them. Don't fake updates.
- Once a page sits in positions 4–15, strengthen it: add a section, a table, an FAQ and two internal links to it.

## 6. Conversions and trust (also ranking signals)

- Google watches whether visitors stay or bounce back to the results. Real photos of your products, your phone number and address, and fast replies all help.
- **Payment methods:** Cash App, Zelle, Chime, Apple Cash and crypto can't be reversed, so many first-time buyers hesitate to send them to a new website. That's expected, and the site explains it openly. Adding even one buyer-protected option later (for example a card processor that accepts bullion merchants) usually lifts conversion noticeably.
- **Keep every promise on the site true.** Insured shipping, adult signature, the 3-day damage window, the authenticity guarantee, the 24-hour price lock and AML checks are all written as your policies. Edit any that don't match how you actually operate (Admin → Pages), because Google's quality raters and customers check them.
